About

A creative space built for sound — professional acoustic treatment, a curated collection of vintage and modern microphones, and the analog warmth that digital alone can't replicate.

Located minutes from Boxhagener Platz in the vibrant Boxi Kiez, Resonance Studio Berlin offers an environment where music is captured with character and intention.
