About

A creative space built for sound — professional acoustic treatment, a curated collection of vintage and modern microphones, and the analog warmth that digital alone can't replicate.

Located minutes from Frankfurter Tor in the vibrant Boxi Kiez, Resonance Studio Berlin offers an environment where music is captured with character and intention.

Services

Recording

From intimate solo sessions to full band tracking. Our control room and live room are designed for pristine capture with character.

Mixing
